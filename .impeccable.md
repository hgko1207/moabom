## Design Context

### Users
- Two children (6 and 4) who watch together on a tablet or phone; the 4-year-old cannot read, so every child-facing control works by picture, number and position.
- A parent who sets the daily limit and which characters show, behind the parent gate; often in a hurry, on a phone.
- Other parents who arrive from Instagram, Naver or KakaoTalk links and decide in seconds whether this looks safe and calm.
- Job: open the app, see the next episode in order, tap and watch; stop kindly when today's time is up.

### Brand Personality
- 따뜻한 그림책 (a warm picture book): calm, kind, unhurried.
- Three words: 포근함, 차분함, 믿음직함.
- The child should feel welcomed; the parent should feel the app is on their side (no tricks, no endless feed).

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light screens only; the moon screen ("오늘은 여기까지") is the one dark, bedtime moment.
- The official thumbnails are the pictures of the book: large, rounded, given room. Chrome stays quiet around them.
- Tokens (src/styles.css :root, SPEC §10): --bg #F3F5EC, --surface #FDFEFB (never pure white), --ink #18221E, --ink-2 #5B6B62, --line #DFE6D6, one accent = the profile colour (#2E9E6B, the brand green, since 2026-10-20) (or a collection colour on its screens), 12px corners (the switch track is the one round exception), 64px touch targets, spacing in multiples of 8, Pretendard only.
- The moon (src/art.ts, 2026-10-20) is the one picture the app owns: a filled gold crescent with one star and a picture-book ledge, no face, used wherever the day is over. Never a stock icon moon.
- Brand mark: play button sprouting from two leaves; brand green #2E9E6B is also the profile accent.
- Anti-references: YouTube Kids' busy feed, neon/dark "gamer" kids apps, emoji as decoration, drawn mascots, purple gradients, glassmorphism, cards inside cards.

### Design Principles
1. Pictures and numbers first; words are for the parent and can be small.
2. One obvious next step on every child screen (the next episode), never a wall of choices.
3. Calm motion: one gentle entrance per screen, nothing that loops or blinks; respect reduced motion.
4. Parent screens read as a quiet list of switches and choices, never as a dashboard. Notices have four looks: 새 소식 = moon-gold paper, 해야 할 일 = accent-outlined card with one filled button, 알려 주기 = a line and words, 문제 = soft red. Filled buttons are the deep accent (--accent-ink) with paper text. Settings = today + a menu; every detail screen opens with a small picture of what the children will see, then rows (SPEC "새 설정을 넣는 규칙").
5. Bedtime is a moment, not an error: the moon screen should feel like closing the book.
